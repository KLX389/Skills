# Editing Checklist

Use this checklist before sending a long, sensitive, or publication-bound edit.

- Meaning preserved: no new facts, promises, examples, numbers, or claims were invented.
- Clutter removed: filler, repetition, inflated transitions, and throat-clearing are gone.
- Verbs improved: nominalizations and passives became direct verbs where accuracy allows it.
- Actors visible: readers can tell who does what, unless the actor is truly unknown or irrelevant.
- Concrete enough: abstract claims are supported by source details or flagged as unsupported.
- Opening works: the first sentence carries substance, tension, or orientation.
- Ending works: the final sentence lands on the point, consequence, or next step.
- Rhythm works aloud: sentence lengths vary and overloaded clauses are split.
- Voice is human: the text sounds competent, not bureaucratic, evasive, or over-marketed.
- Mode followed: feedback-only and direct-rewrite requests contain no extra sections.
